About Me

Researcher & Software Engineer

I am a predoctoral researcher (PhD student) in the Department of Computer Engineering and Mathematics at Universitat Rovira i Virgili (URV), Tarragona, Spain. My research focuses on the intersection of Computer Vision, Artificial Intelligence, and Aquaculture, where I develop intelligent systems for automated fish behavior analysis and reproduction prediction.

My PhD work applies deep learning models—including object detection, tracking, and image classification—to study the reproductive behavior of Senegalese sole (Solea senegalensis). This interdisciplinary research has been featured in international media and presented at major European aquaculture conferences.

Prior to my PhD, I gained 6+ years of professional experience as a Senior Android Developer, building scalable mobile applications in the finance, e-commerce, and energy sectors. I bring a unique combination of academic rigor and industry expertise to my work.
